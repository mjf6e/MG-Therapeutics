Company overview

A gene therapy platform built on decades of membrane-repair biology.

MG Therapeutics is a biotechnology company developing a first-in-class gene therapy to restore neuromuscular junction integrity in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, a devastating neurodegenerative disease with limited treatment options.

Primary focus

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), targeting the neuromuscular junction and the diaphragm to preserve respiratory function.

Proprietary platform

Dual gene delivery of MG53 and MG29 via next-generation MyoAAV vectors, engineered for systemic, muscle-selective expression.

Where things stand

Preclinical validation in ALS models, a growing intellectual property portfolio, and a leadership team of key opinion leaders driving toward the clinic.

Scientific rationale

ALS follows a "dying-back" pattern, and it starts at the membrane.

Peripheral neuromuscular junction dysfunction precedes motor neuron loss. MG Therapeutics targets that earlier point of failure.

Early NMJ degeneration

ALS involves early degeneration of the neuromuscular junction, particularly in the diaphragm, well before overt motor neuron loss.

Membrane repair failure

ALS-affected muscle shows membrane repair failure and oxidative stress, both of which drive disease progression.

Two proteins, two roles

MG53 aggregation and MG29 dysfunction are both common pathological features found in human muscle in ALS.

MG53: a molecular bandage

A naturally occurring regenerative protein that nucleates the machinery cells use to reseal injured membranes. Circulating MG53, acting as a myokine, plays a role in repairing injury to the diaphragm and NMJ, and supports regenerative capacity in other tissues under chronic oxidative stress.

MG29: structural integrity

A muscle-specific synaptophysin-family protein essential to t-tubule structure and calcium signaling. In ALS-affected muscle, MG29 helps maintain the structural integrity of the disrupted t-tubule network and supports diaphragm metabolic function.

The platform

MyoAAV-tPA-MG53-MG29

A synergistic strategy that pairs MG53's membrane-repair function with MG29's structural role, delivered systemically through a muscle-selective vector.

01

Beyond AAV9

MyoAAV overcomes key limitations of AAV9: off-target liver expression and poor muscle transduction.

02

Systemic delivery

A single systemic administration is designed to reach skeletal muscle and the diaphragm directly.

03

Dual expression

Drives high circulating MG53 and MG29 expression in skeletal muscle, restoring diaphragm membrane integrity.

04

Selective by design

Preclinical work shows no off-target expression in the CNS or liver, alongside improved motor reflexes and activity.

Proof of concept: recombinant MG53 in ALS mice

Published, peer-reviewed work with recombinant human MG53 protein (rhMG53) supports the biological rationale behind the platform.

rhMG53 localizes to injured diaphragm muscle, forming repair patches and reducing dye uptake at the injury site.

rhMG53 enhances neuromuscular junction integrity and preserves motor neurons in the spinal cord.

These effects translate into improved survival and respiratory function in ALS mouse models.
